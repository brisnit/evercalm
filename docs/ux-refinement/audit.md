# EverCalm UX and visual-design audit

Read-only review of the complete product before the refinement pass, 15
September 2026.

## How it was reviewed

`tests/e2e/screenshots-ux.spec.ts` signed in as each persona and walked every
major route at **desktop (1440×900)** and **iPhone 14 (390×844)**, saving a
full-page screenshot and measuring each screen. Screenshots and measurements are
in `docs/screenshots/ux/before/` (gitignored). Every screen was also read in
code.

| Persona        | Role                             | Screens |
| -------------- | -------------------------------- | ------: |
| Visitor        | Public site                      |       5 |
| Dana Okafor    | Harbor & Vine owner              |      39 |
| Marcus Bell    | Riverside general manager        |      12 |
| Ava Lindqvist  | New server, onboarding           |      12 |
| Sam Whitfield  | Server, on shift                 |      12 |
| Ana Beltrán    | Lumen Salon owner (manual pilot) |       9 |
| Elodie Garnier | New stylist, onboarding          |      12 |
| Morgan Ellis   | EverCalm support administrator   |       4 |
| Jamie          | EverCalm support agent           |       4 |

**Baseline, measured across all 218 captures:** every screen returned 200; **0**
axe WCAG 2.2 AA violations; **0** screens with sideways overflow; **0** console
errors. The foundations are sound. The problems below are about hierarchy,
navigation, density and consistency, not broken pages.

Other measurements that drive the findings:

- 21 of 109 phone screens are taller than three phone screens; six are taller
  than six (home 13,224px, audit log 7,779px, Marcus's schedule 7,193px, Ava's
  shift 7,000px, Marcus's operations 6,667px, Sam's shift 5,772px).
- 240 interactive targets smaller than 24px on phone, on 74 screens (inline
  text links, "← Back" links at 17px tall, 16px checkboxes).
- Screens with three or more competing primary actions: Ava's shift (16), Sam's
  shift (11), schedule requests (4), Elodie's home and onboarding (3).
- The code repeats instead of sharing: 15 private `SELECT` style constants, 18
  hand-styled textareas, 11 hand-built primary button links, 16 hand-built
  secondary button links. There is no `loading.tsx`, `error.tsx` or
  `not-found.tsx` anywhere.

Severity: **Critical** blocks or misleads a core task · **High** slows a
frequent task or undermines trust · **Medium** friction or inconsistency ·
**Low** polish. Scope: **Shared** means a component, shell or token change;
**Screen** means one page.

## Order of work

1. **Phase 1, shared system:** tokens and app ground, `ButtonLink`/`TextLink`,
   `Select`/`Textarea`, `BackLink`, active navigation, phone admin menu,
   employee bottom navigation, `StatTile` quiet zeros, `PageHeader` without
   redundant eyebrows, enum labels, `ProgressRing` ids, loading/error/not-found
   states, permission-denied copy. Fixes C5, N1–N6, H3–H4, I1, I4, M1–M3,
   A1–A4, S1, X1–X4, B1.
2. **Phase 2, employee:** C2, C3, C4, H6, I5, M4, S4, B2.
3. **Phase 3, manager and owner:** C1, H1, H2, H5, I2, I3, N7, S2, B3.
4. **Phase 4, EverCalm team:** attention-first organization list, readable
   labels, role-aware actions (X5, S3).
5. **Phase 5, marketing:** N2, motion, lifestyle image placements (B4, P3).
